<p class="poem">
~<br><br>

before me, a billion sparkling lights<br>
brought to life an otherwise empty night.<br>
though they appeared no farther than a finger's width apart,<br>
they would never touch, for space was too sparse.<br><br>

regardless of this, every so often,<br>
the lights have their ways of forming a connection.<br>
with perfect positions, at just the right time,<br>
they suspend in the sky in a perfect line.<br><br>

but the lights, for how beautifully they shined,<br>
couldn't hold a candle to what lay before my eyes.<br>
among all cosmic coincidences, the luckiest one,<br>
was the fateful alignment between your eyes and mine.<br><br>

in this moment, with your light by my side,<br>
the rest in the sky seemed to dance with color.<br>
i thought i was crazy.<br>
but you saw it too, a future like no other.<br><br>

but what lay beyond such a vibrant show<br>
was the ghost of a flame, burnt out long ago.<br>
what lay before me, i just couldn't see past, but alas<br>
it was only a matter of time.<br><br>

even now, though we're worlds apart,<br>
i still search for your hue in others' sparks.<br>
for as long as i live under the same starry night,<br>
i'll be reminded of you, and your undying light.<br><br>

~
</p>
