<p>
I love row/column-forming games like <i>Tetris</i> and <i>Block Blast</i>, since despite being so simple in concept, I find them surprisingly fun to waste time on. So, I decided to start making something on my own. So far, what I have is a basic mixture of these two games.<br>
</p>
<ul>
<li>I use the basic square grid, no-gravity setting that <i>Block Blast</i> also uses.</li>
<li>Like <i>Tetris</i>, all of the blocks are currently tetronimos.</li>
<li>Like <i>Tetris</i>, there is an ability to store one piece at a time.</li>
<li>The piece generation is purely random. The lack of pure RNG in <i>Block Blast</i> frustrated me, and was actually my main motivator for making my own version.</li>
</ul>
<p>
Soon, I hope to add more game structure and special abilities in order to make this otherwise very simple game into something a bit more nuanced and fleshed out. I love the idea of introducing more long-term strategy into this type of game.
</p>
